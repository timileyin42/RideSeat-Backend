01

Who We Are

Data Controller: Rideway Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales.

Contact: privacy@rideway.co.uk

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data protection authority. If you have concerns about how we handle your data, you have the right to contact the ICO at ico.org.uk.

02

Data We Collect

Account & Identity

  • Full name, email address, date of birth
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Phone number (for SMS verification)
  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)

Identity Verification (Drivers only)

  • Driving licence number and photos (front and back)
  • Government-issued ID document photo
  • Selfie photograph for identity matching

Trip & Location Data

  • Origin and destination cities, addresses, and coordinates
  • Departure times and trip history

Payment Information

  • Payment is processed securely by Stripe — we do not store card numbers or CVVs
  • For drivers: bank account details (sort code, account number) provided to Stripe for payouts
  • Transaction records (amounts, dates, booking references)

Communications

  • In-app messages between drivers and passengers
  • Support ticket content
  • Reviews and ratings

Device & Technical Data

  • Device identifier and platform (iOS/Android) for push notifications
  • App version and IP address

04

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and maintain your Rideway account
  • To facilitate trip bookings between drivers and passengers
  • To process payments and transfer earnings to drivers
  • To verify driver identity before allowing them to offer rides
  • To send you notifications about bookings, payments, and trip updates
  • To enable in-app messaging between matched drivers and passengers
  • To investigate disputes and provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To send you marketing communications (only if you have opted in)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

International Transfers

Some providers (Stripe, Google, Apple) are based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards under UK GDPR Article 46 — specifically UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) or equivalent Standard Contractual Clauses with each provider.

You can request details of transfer mechanisms by emailing privacy@rideway.co.uk.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights:

✅ Right of Access
Request a copy of all data we hold about you (Subject Access Request).
✎️ Right to Rectification
Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
🗑️ Right to Erasure
Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
📦 Right to Portability
Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
⛔ Right to Object
Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
🔒 Right to Restriction
Restrict how we use your data while a dispute is resolved.
↩️ Withdraw Consent
Withdraw marketing consent at any time.
🏛️ Right to Complain
Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk or call 0303 123 1113.

Email privacy@rideway.co.uk to exercise any right. We respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account in-app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

09

Cookies & Tracking

The Rideway mobile app does not use cookies. It uses device storage to retain authentication tokens locally on your device.

If you visit rideway.co.uk, our website may use essential cookies for security and session management only. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

10

Children

Rideway is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from under-18s. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, contact privacy@rideway.co.uk and we will delete it promptly.

11

Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are hashed — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Payment card data is handled entirely by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1) — we do not store card numbers
  • Authentication tokens expire after 30 minutes with secure refresh token rotation
  • Identity verification documents are stored in encrypted, access-controlled storage

In the event of a data breach posing risk to individuals, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and affected users without undue delay, as required by UK GDPR Article 33.
