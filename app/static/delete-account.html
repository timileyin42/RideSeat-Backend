Delete Your Rideway Account

You can delete your account directly in the app or by submitting a request below. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

📱
Delete in the App
Fastest method — processed immediately
  1. Open the Rideway app on your phone
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation)
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete my account
⚠ Before you delete: Cancel any upcoming trips or bookings first to avoid impacting other users.
✉️
Submit a Deletion Request
If you can't access the app — we process within 30 days
By submitting you confirm you are the account holder. We verify your identity before processing. We aim to complete requests within 30 days as required by UK GDPR.
